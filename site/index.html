System Architecture

How Drive Shield Works

A real-time 3-step pipeline designed to sense surrounding hazards, arbitrate urgency, and deliver immediate driver warnings.

STEP 01 // INPUT

SENSE

Continuous multi-modal data acquisition:

  • 2 wide-angle cameras (front windshield & rear glass)
  • 4 ultrasonic sensors (front & rear bumper corners)
  • 6-axis motion sensor (gyroscope & accelerometer)
  • Car speed over OBD-II (read-only diagnostic port)
  • GPS positioning & 4G cellular module
STEP 02 // EDGE COMPUTE

DECIDE

Edge processing on Raspberry Pi 5:

  • Reads camera frames and acoustic ultrasonic distances
  • Correlates road vision with live OBD-II vehicle speed
  • Runs collision and lane departure prediction logic
  • Applies debounce and hysteresis to prevent alert flicker
  • Arbitrates priorities and picks exactly ONE alert to show
STEP 03 // OUTPUT

WARN

Instantaneous multi-sensory notification:

  • High-contrast 7-inch driver dashboard screen
  • Dynamic acoustic beeps via dedicated dashboard speaker
  • Synchronized visual icon and audible beep cadence
  • Cloud hazard sharing for confirmed road risks
  • Driver retains 100% mechanical authority at all times

Feature Catalog

What Drive Shield Warns About

18 distinct warning capabilities designed for older vehicles. Filter by zone to see how vision, acoustics, and road telemetry protect the driver.

Forward Collision Warning

Critical

Monitors closing distance and relative deceleration against vehicles ahead. Warns driver when time to collision drops below safety margins.

Sensors: Front Camera + OBD-II Speed

Lane Departure Warning

Advisory

Tracks painted lane markings and vehicle lateral position. Warns if the car drifts over lane lines without an active turn indicator.

Sensors: Front Camera

Pedestrian & Cyclist Detection

Critical

Detects vulnerable road users crossing or travelling along the vehicle's trajectory in urban driving and low-light conditions.

Sensors: Front Camera AI Vision

Road Edge Detection

Advisory

Identifies unpaved road shoulders, gravel berms, and earthen ditches on Indian rural highways where painted lines are missing.

Sensors: Front Camera

Turning Collision Warning

Critical

Evaluates approaching oncoming traffic and pedestrian paths when executing right-hand or left-hand turns across lanes.

Sensors: Front Camera + Motion Sensor

Intersection Assistance

Advisory

Scans blind cross-junctions and roundabouts for cross-cutting traffic that enters without yielding right of way.

Sensors: Front Camera + GPS

Speed-Sign Cross-Check

★ Unique

Reads posted speed limit road signs via vision and verifies against the car's real vehicle speed read over the OBD-II diagnostic port.

Sensors: Front Camera + OBD-II Speed

Over-Speed Alert

Advisory

Notifies the driver when exceeding national or expressway statutory limits, helping prevent high-speed collisions.

Sensors: OBD-II Speed + GPS Limits

Curve Speed Advice

Advisory

Advises safe approach velocities for sharp hairpin bends and ghat road curves using GPS trajectory data and road signs.

Sensors: GPS + Front Camera

Blind-Spot Warning

Advisory

Monitors the rear-quarter zones of the car to advise the driver of vehicles cruising in the side mirror blind spot.

Sensors: Rear Camera + Rear Ultrasonics

Lane-Change Assist

Critical

Warns if a vehicle in the adjacent lane is rapidly closing in from behind when the driver begins an overtaking manoeuvre.

Sensors: Rear Camera Vision

Rear Cross-Traffic Alert

Critical

Warns of approaching cars, motorbikes, or cyclists coming from the side when reversing out of perpendicular parking bays.

Sensors: Rear Camera + Ultrasonics

Rear Collision Warning

Critical

Monitors fast-closing tailgating vehicles behind and alerts the driver to prepare for an impact or move forward if safe.

Sensors: Rear Camera

Safe Exit Assist

Advisory

Warns passengers opening doors after parking if a cyclist, motorbike, or car is passing alongside the vehicle.

Sensors: Rear Camera Vision

Front & Rear Parking Sensors

Advisory

Advisory

Driver Human Factors

Three Strict Alert Levels

To prevent driver panic and cognitive overload, only ONE alert is shown and heard at any moment. The highest urgency level always takes precedence.

Level 1 // Safe

All Clear

Green indicator, gentle ambient status. No acoustic disturbance. System passively tracks trajectories and road conditions.

TTC: ≥ 3.0 s | Audio: Silence
Level 2 // Caution

Advisory Alert

Amber visual glow on dashboard screen. Gentle sine tone (523 Hz, 180 ms soft fade) sounding once per second to draw driver attention early.

TTC: < 3.0 s | Audio: 1 gentle beep/s
Level 3 // Warning

Critical Alert

Flashing high-contrast red warning (flashes 2.5 times/s). Harsh square wave tone (1600 Hz, 5 beeps/s) demanding immediate driver manual braking.

TTC: < 2.2 s | Audio: 5 harsh beeps/s

Debounce: Requires 3 consecutive matching camera samples before triggering, preventing single-frame sensor noise from sounding false alarms.
Hysteresis (+0.3 s margin): Once triggered, Critical warning requires TTC to rise above 2.5 s (2.2 + 0.3) to clear; Advisory requires TTC above 3.3 s (3.0 + 0.3) to clear, eliminating erratic on/off audio chattering.

Retrofit Architecture

Where Each Part Goes

Designed for non-invasive mounting on older cars. Tap any numbered hotspot on the vehicle diagram or click the hardware pills below to inspect mounting details.

01 // Mounting Point

Front Camera (Pi Camera Module 3)

Physical Location: Behind the rearview mirror, mounted on windshield
Sensor Coverage: 120° forward field of view
Functional Role: Captures road scene, forward vehicles, lane markings, pedestrians, road signs, and road edges.

⚠️ Open Issues & Practical Packaging Limits

  • Rear camera cable length: The standard CSI ribbon cable supplied with the camera is too short to reach the rear tailgate of a car; an extended differential CSI-to-HDMI or FFC extension cable is required.
  • Blind-spot coverage geometry: Ultrasonic sensors placed on bumper corners monitor only the rear-quarter zones; there is no side coverage along the middle vehicle doors.
  • Physical mounting constraints: Retrofitting ultrasonic sensors requires drilling bumper holes or custom bracket fabrication; dashboard screen and camera mounts require heat-resistant adhesive or mechanical clamps.

Target Beneficiaries

Who Drive Shield Is For

Designed to bridge the technology gap for vehicles that are mechanically sound but lack modern electronic warnings.

Owners of Older Cars

Drivers keeping their reliable 10-to-13-year-old hatchback or sedan who want modern safety warnings without the heavy financial burden of buying a new vehicle.

Used-Car Dealers

Pre-owned automotive dealerships looking to enhance the safety appeal and market value of certified pre-owned vehicles.

Taxi & Fleet Operators

Commercial fleet managers and cab services seeking reduced accident risk and lower insurance claims across high-mileage older fleets.

COMPATIBILITY REQUIREMENT

Works only on cars with a usable OBD-II port (roughly 2013 onward); older cars depend on the model.

Transparency & Limitations

Engineering Limits

We are upfront about what Drive Shield cannot do. Safety systems require unambiguous, honest boundaries.

Driver Authority: You Stay in Control

Drive Shield is an SAE Level 0 advisory concept. It never brakes the wheels and never steers the vehicle. The human driver remains 100% responsible for driving at all times.

Adverse Weather & Vision Limits

Optical cameras struggle in darkness, torrential rain, thick fog, headlight glare, and on faded lane markings. However, ultrasonic sensors, motion sensors, and OBD-II vehicle speed continue operating.

No Road Testing Yet

Results and performance figures so far are simulated in computer models. No physical hardware has been built and no vehicle has been road-tested.

Not a Certified Safety Device

Drive Shield is an academic student concept from NIIT University. It has not undergone automotive regulatory approval, AIS compliance, or ARAI certification.

PRIVACY ARCHITECTURE

Location data is used only for hazard sharing, strictly with explicit user consent. No persistent journey logs or personal tracking data are collected or transmitted.

Future Development Plan

Project Concept Roadmap

A structured path for moving from concept simulation to physical prototyping. Notice: Automatic braking and steering are strictly excluded from our scope.

Phase 1 // Data

Real-Road Recording & Tuning

Capture driving video and CAN bus telemetry on varied Indian roads to tune debounce timers, warning distances, and hysteresis thresholds.

Phase 2 // Vision

Indian Road Vision Models

Train lightweight edge neural networks on local Indian traffic scenarios, including auto-rickshaws, cattle, unmarked speed breakers, and chaotic lanes.

Phase 3 // Network

Open Hazard Network

Establish an open crowdsourced protocol that any vehicle can connect to through an optional smartphone app or data partnerships.

Phase 4 // Monitoring

Driver-Fatigue Warning

Explore an optional cabin-facing camera to advise drowsy drivers when micro-sleep or eyelid closure is detected (with driver consent).

Phase 5 // Fleet

Fleet Telemetry Dashboard

Build aggregated hazard risk mapping for commercial fleet operators with explicit consent to help plan safer delivery corridors.

Phase 6 // Optimization

Cost Reduction Engineering

Redesign compute hardware onto specialized automotive microcontrollers and custom PCBs to bring manufacturing costs below Rs 30,000.
